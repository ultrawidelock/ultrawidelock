Why it is different

Distance is measured, not trusted.

Signal strength can be faked from across the street. Time of flight cannot: light takes 3.34 ns per metre, and nothing makes it arrive early.

  • Time of flight, not signal strength

    A DS-TWR round under a scrambled timestamp sequence puts the phone on a ruler with 4.7 mm ticks. A relay can only add delay, which moves the phone further from the door.

  • Verified on the lock

    The credential is checked on the device itself. No app in the loop, no account, no cloud round trip, and therefore no remote verifier to compromise.

  • The phone already in your pocket

    An iPhone or Apple Watch opens it on approach over UWB or on tap over NFC, and the lock reports its state to Apple Home over Matter.

How a door opens

Learned from the air.
Written by hand.

Five steps in the order they happen, each one studied as it crossed the air and reimplemented in portable C: the credential exchange, the key ladder, STS, DS-TWR, and a Matter node without CHIP. The notes are public.

  1. 01BLE The phone finds the lock’s credential service.
  2. 02Authenticate AUTH0, AUTH1 and EXCHANGE, and both ends hold the ranging key.
  3. 03Range The key ladder seeds an STS session, then one DS-TWR round.
  4. 04Gate A trusted range inside 1.00 m moves the bolt.
  5. 05Home Lock state into Apple Home over Thread or Wi-Fi.

On real hardware

Three chip families. One portable core.

  • DWM3001CDK nRF52833 + DW3110

    The reference board. Radio, MCU and a J-Link on one part, nothing to wire. Matter over Thread. Also builds without Zephyr.

  • nRF5340 DK DWM3000EVB + NFC12A1

    UWB and NFC on the same lock, so a tap works when a walk-up does not. Matter over Thread.

  • ESP32-S3 / C5 / C6 DWM3000EVB

    Matter over Wi-Fi, and the tier the web flasher installs with no toolchain at all.
